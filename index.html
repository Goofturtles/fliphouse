How the numbers work

Finding flips

Every item is grouped with its market twins — reforges stripped, stars bucketed, pets bucketed by level, books matched by enchant. Then the important part: a flip only counts against listings whose lore actually matches (enchants, dyes, gems, upgrades), so a clean item is never "flipped" against a maxed one. Sells/day comes from real sales sampled every minute — demand numbers sharpen the longer you keep the tab open.

Tax is already deducted

Profit shown is after the auction house claiming tax: 1% under 10m, 2% from 10m to 100m, 2.5% above. What you see is what lands in your purse.

Risk badges

solid real sold prices back the resale, and the buy sits well under them. fair sales back it with a thinner edge — or a deep, tight market still awaiting sale confirmation. risky unverified — no real sales seen yet, asks far above what buyers pay, or a margin that smells like hidden-value bait.
